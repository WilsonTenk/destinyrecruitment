## English

### About us
**We choose who enters your home the way we'd choose for our own**

Destiny Recruitment is a Riyadh agency providing trained, qualified staff for families, schools and companies: nannies, cooks, cleaners, caregivers and hospitality staff.

For years we have helped families across Riyadh find people they can trust with their homes, their children and their elderly. We started with a simple idea: every home should feel safe with the person who walks through its door. Today we treat every request as if it were for our own home — with honesty, care and clarity from the very first message.

### How we prepare our staff
- **Selection:** We interview every worker ourselves and check her experience and documents before recommending her to any home.
- **Training:** Before starting, staff are trained in cleaning, cooking, childcare basics and how to work respectfully with a family.
- **Follow-up:** We stay in touch with you and the worker after she starts. Any concern you send us on WhatsApp is handled right away.

### Replacement guarantee
Your peace of mind comes first. If the worker isn't the right fit within the first 3 days, we replace her at no extra cost.
- **Guarantee period:** 3 days
- **When it applies:** not a good fit for the family, or not committed to the work
- **How to ask:** one WhatsApp message

### FAQ
- **How long does booking take?** We usually reply within an hour and arrange a start date based on the service and availability.
- **How can I pay?** Cash, bank transfer or STC Pay.
- **Can I book by the hour?** Yes – hourly, monthly, or longer contracts, whatever you need.
- **Do you serve companies and schools?** Yes – cleaners and hospitality staff for schools, offices and events.
