Our story

Built on trust

Destiny Recruitment is a Riyadh agency providing trained, qualified staff for families, schools and companies: nannies, cooks, cleaners, caregivers and hospitality staff.

For years we have helped families across Riyadh find people they can trust with their homes, their children and their elderly. Every request is treated as if it were for our own home.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Message us and we’ll reply right away.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does booking take?

We reply right away and arrange a start date based on the service and availability.

How much does it cost?

There’s no fixed price list. The price depends on your home (type, size and outdoor areas), the services you combine, the schedule and who needs care. Send us a request and we’ll agree one clear price with you.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer or STC Pay.

Can I book by the hour?

Yes – hourly, monthly, or longer contracts, whatever you need.

Do you serve companies and schools?

Yes – cleaners and hospitality staff for schools, offices and events.
