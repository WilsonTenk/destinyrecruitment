How pricing works

Every home is different, so is every price

There’s no fixed price list. We look at your home and what you need, then agree one clear price with you, for one service or a full blend.

Home type & size

Apartment, villa or compound, and how many rooms.

Outdoor & compound areas

Gardens, yards, majlis and shared compound spaces.

Services combined

One service or a blend, like a nanny, a cook and a cleaner together.

Hours & schedule

A few hours, part-time, full month or live-in.

Who needs care

Number of children, and elderly or sick family members.

Area & start date

Where you are in Riyadh and when you need us to start.
